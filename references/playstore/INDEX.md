# Google Play Developer Program Policies — Index

Policy version: **July 2026, plus the October 29, 2026 policy update** (source: https://play.google/developer-content-policy/). Load only the file(s) you need.

**How Play policies differ from Apple's guidelines:** Play policies are organized by **named policy areas**, not numbered sections. Many requirements are enforced through **Play Console declarations and forms** (Data safety, Permissions Declaration, Financial Features, Health apps, Target audience & content, gambling application) rather than only through binary review. Enforcement covers the app, its metadata, its ads, all third-party SDKs inside it, AND the developer account with its whole catalog. Individual policy pages live under https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/.

## October 29, 2026 policy update (source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18100766)

New: Local Network permission (declaration for API 37+), Manage Contacts permission (declaration), AppFunctions API rules. Updated: eligibility-based subscription discounts (student plans) disclosures. Clarified: Location (minimum scope, location button for API 37+, fine-location declaration — mandatory Jan 27, 2027), All Files Access, Accessibility API, Deceptive Behavior (review manipulation), Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs, Cryptocurrency (Hong Kong), SMS/Call Log (phone-call verification banned from Jan 27, 2027), Payments for agentic purchases. Reminders: Play Age Signals API, Japan payments authorization (Dec 1, 2026), trader status declaration (Nov 2026), memory/code-optimization quality thresholds (Feb 2027). Details are appended to the relevant area files under "Update (announced October 29, 2026…)".
